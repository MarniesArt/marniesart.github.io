About
Me

Thanks for looking ♡

My name is Amarni Stephenson. I enjoy painting, photography, and creating and designing clothing. I have explored these ideas through my projects at college, where I have mostly explored surrealism in art and feelings of expression.

I live in the little town of Upton upon Severn, a scenic, historic place that has nurtured my interest in older-fashioned styles, particularly the 1960s and 1970s. Its bustling events, such as the blues and summer festivals, fill the streets with music and fashion.

I hope to one day make and design clothes to sell, and for my designs to appear in fashion magazines, be advertised, and become popular around the world.

Inspiration notes

Artists who inspire me.

01 - 03

01 / music & style

Lana Del Rey

Lana Del Rey inspires me because her vintage Americana incorporates 1950s and 1960s fashion, while her music focuses on real-life struggles. I love the meaning of her songs and her calm, elegant singing style, which matches the colour themes of her album covers.

Lust for Life feels soft and elegant, yet still explores struggles while keeping an upbeat mood. I find this balance in her other albums too, including Norman F***ing Rockwell!, Born to Die, and Did You Know That There's a Tunnel Under Ocean Blvd.

While Did You Know That There's a Tunnel Under Ocean Blvd keeps an elegant, upbeat style, it focuses more deeply on personal struggles. By bringing personal scenarios into the open, it helps listeners feel heard and seen, which makes me feel connected to this artist.

02 / fashion & form

Iris van Herpen

I also really like Iris van Herpen because her work investigates body image and real-life problems. I admire her fashion style and the unique designs she produces.

She makes distorted dresses using linework and patterns to create the illusion of a distorted body. This shifts the focus away from what a "perfect" body should look like and instead shows the bare, raw truth that everybody is different: there is no single perfect body or beauty standard.

03 / photography & feeling

Juno Calypso

Juno Calypso inspires me because she is a photographer who focuses on feminism, isolation, loneliness, self-sufficiency, and body image. These themes are shown throughout her photographs.

She is an independent artist, and I love the style of her work: her colour themes and camera angles make the meanings clear and visible. Her style is unique, showing what it can feel like to be a woman when young and older, and what it feels like to grow older and feel stuck.
